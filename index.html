BusyLight

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10-09-2026

BusyLight ("the app") is developed by Gustav Nobel. This policy explains what data the app accesses, why, and — just as importantly — what it does not do with it.

Summary

BusyLight has no backend server. It runs entirely on your phone and talks only to (a) the BusyLight hardware accessory over Bluetooth, and (b) Microsoft's own servers, directly, only if you choose to connect Microsoft Teams. The developer never receives, stores, or has access to any of your data.

What the app accesses, and why

  • Bluetooth — to find, connect to, and control your paired BusyLight accessory. Never used to determine your location.
  • Phone call state — to detect when you're on a call and show that on the light. The app does not read or store phone numbers or call history.
  • Notifications — read only to detect call-style notifications from apps like WhatsApp or Signal, so the light can reflect those calls too. Notification content is never stored or transmitted anywhere.
  • Calendar — only the calendars you explicitly select in Settings, and only to check whether you currently have a busy event. Calendar data stays on your device.
  • Usage access — used only to detect when a meeting app you've selected (e.g. Zoom, Google Meet) is on screen, as another automatic busy signal. The app does not collect your broader app usage history.
  • Microsoft Teams (optional) — if you choose to connect Teams, the app signs you in directly with Microsoft using the standard OAuth device-code flow and periodically checks your presence status via the Microsoft Graph API, using your own Microsoft account credentials. This connection is directly between your phone and Microsoft's servers; BusyLight's developer never sees this data. You can disconnect at any time from Settings, which deletes the stored sign-in token from your device.

What the app does not do

  • It does not run ads or include any advertising SDK.
  • It does not use analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs of any kind.
  • It does not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party, other than the direct Microsoft Teams connection you explicitly opt into.
  • It does not require an account to use the core features (calls, notifications, calendar, meeting-app detection).

Data retention and deletion

Because there is no server, there is no remotely stored data to delete. Signing out of Teams removes the locally stored access token immediately. Uninstalling the app removes all remaining local data, including your Settings preferences.

Children's privacy

BusyLight is a workplace productivity accessory app and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly. Material changes will be reflected in the app's Play Store listing.
